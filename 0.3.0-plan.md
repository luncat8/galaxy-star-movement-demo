# 0.3.0 plan — kinematic aligned ICs (S2) + color grading

Goal: grand-design arms span the whole visible disk at settle, by biasing the
initial epicycle phases to the same winding law as the potential, in the pattern
frame. Inside CR the forcing locks the alignment (persistent); outside CR
alignment decorrelates over the measured shear times (~20-70 tu) — an honest
transient unwind. Also: warmer core / bluer arms in the glow and stars.

## 1. S2 kinematic aligned ICs

### Physics
- In the linear epicycle picture each star has a guiding center (Rg, phig),
  radial epicycle amplitude a = e * Rg, and epicycle phase psi.
- For m=2 spiral response, forced orbits tilt so their apocenters pile up on
  the spiral crest inside CR; outside CR no steady forced orbit exists (the
  linear response is damped/sheared), so the present ICs are axisymmetric there
  and the render reads as scatter.
- S2 seeds a coherent epicyclic eccentricity e_align(R) with apocenter angle
  psi_align(R) = phi_spiral(R) (same winding law) at t=0, ON TOP of the existing
  random velocity dispersion. Think of it as starting the disk already in the
  approximate forced-response configuration everywhere. Inside CR the forcing
  maintains it; outside CR orbits shear apart over the measured t_decorr.
- This is IC only: same leapfrog, same potential, same gates. Energy/Jacobi
  diagnostics reset after settle; orbits remain honest.

### e_align(R) profile
- R < R_in = 1.0: zero (inside ILR bar region, forcing already organizes).
- R_in .. R_peak = 2.2 (mid-forced zone, near ILR-CR midpoint): rise linearly
  to e_peak = 0.10 chosen to match the m=2 narrow-annulus amplitude already
  present at settle in the forced zone (check-arm-profile shows amp ~0.28-0.46
  at Om=0.30; e_peak=0.10 reproduces that level of ellipse crowding).
- R_peak .. R_out = 6.0: exponential taper e(R) = e_peak * exp(-(R-R_peak)/R_tau)
  with R_tau=1.6. Tapers but does not vanish — outer disk keeps a visible
  aligned ellipse field that unwinds over ~30-70 tu per curve-precession.js.
- Thick disk: half amplitude (warmer, less responsive). Young disk: 1.2x (colder,
  sharper arms). Bulge/halo/stream: unchanged (not disk orbits).

### Implementation
- New function alignEpicycles(st, P, om) called at the END of initStars (before
  computeAccel), guarded by P.align (default 1; allows turning off for gates).
- For each thin/young/thick star:
   * Compute guiding center Rg = current R (good enough for small e).
   * Current velocity: decompose into (vR, vp) in polar frame.
   * Desired epicycle kick in the rotating (pattern) frame:
     - To put apocenter along phi_spiral(R) at t=0, the star must be at
       apocenter of its epicycle ellipse: that means radial velocity vR = 0
       and the star is at R > Rg (outward along major axis).
     - In linear epicycle at apocenter: vR = 0, vp = vc(Rg) - (kappa/(2Omega)) * a * kappa?
       Simpler / exact for small e: on a closed elliptical orbit the apocenter
       has purely tangential velocity vp = v_circ(R_apocenter) * (Rg/R_apocenter)
       by angular momentum conservation, but we want to inject only a small
       perturbation on top of existing random velocities.
   * Recipe: rotate the star into the frame aligned with the spiral crest at
     its azimuth (in pattern frame). Add a small radial + tangential velocity
     kick and a small radial position shift corresponding to an epicycle of
     amplitude e(Rg)*Rg whose apocenter points along the crest. Superimpose on
     existing velocities (so random dispersions remain) but subtract mean vR
     drift so there's no net radial flow.
- After kick: re-circularize at the 5% level by clipping extreme velocities;
  call computeAccel once at the end (leapfrog needs a(x,t) on entry).

### Gates / validation
- check-response, check-jacobi (run with P.align=0 in legacy mode — physics
  unchanged), check-no-clumping all PASS.
- check-arm-profile with align=1: at settle, m=2 amp >= 0.18 out to R~5 and
  coherent phase (dpsi within ±40° of spiral winding) across all annuli 1-6.
  At T=150: inner (R<3.5) amp still >= 0.2; outer (R>4) amp may drop but T=150
  morphology must not look worse than current (i.e., glow still coherent, no
  clumping).
- Headless renders render-png.js: settle shows full-disk arms; T=75 shows arms
  winding out; T=150 shows inner arms persistent + outer softened but not noise.

## 2. Warm-core / bluer-arm color

### Star colors
- Thin disk: #fff4e0 -> #ffe8c8 (slightly warmer/yellower old pop)
- Young disk: #7fb8ff -> #6faaff (bluer, brighter, the arm tracer)
- Thick disk: slightly warmer; bulge more orange-red
- Halo belt: cool blue-gray unchanged
- Sprite gradient: core white -> class color -> transparent, no change.

### Glow color gradient
Instead of uniform (170,200,255) blue, modulate color by radius in the pattern
frame (using gAcc intensity + local R estimate):
- Inner R < 1.5: warm yellow-white (255, 225, 180)
- Mid R 1.5-3.5: neutral white (220, 225, 240)
- Outer R > 3.5: bluer (160, 195, 255)
- The DoG excess (arms) gets a bluer tint than the isotropic base (which
  follows the radial gradient).
- Implementation: while filling gImg.data, look up gx,gy -> (xp,yp) -> R_pattern
  (de-rotated); interpolate warm->cool color. Arm excess shifts blue; base
  follows radial gradient.

### Core light
- The central radial gradient already has warm tint; boost its alpha slightly
  and extend the warm hue.
