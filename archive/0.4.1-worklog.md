# 0.4.1 worklog — outer-band transient capture (S2b)

## Goal

Capture the time history of the outer band (armed epochs, birth/death, one-armed
vs two-armed) at N=10k and 40k and decide by archive/0.4.1-plan.md §3: if the
armed epochs correlate with the live term's phase coherence at R 4.5 → tune the
feedback (tau 8-20, weaker cap, slow gfb ramp); if they are internal (transient
winding of the S2 field) → IC-side fix S2b (seed the outer disk at the settle
boundary) and keep gfb=0.

## Instrument — experiments/check-transient.js

- 61 samples of 2 tu over T=30..150 (gaps allowed to merge): m=2 phasors in
  bands 4.0-5.0 (outer), 4.5-5.5, 2.5-4.0 (mid), 1.0-2.5 (inner), plus the

  peakA1 (one-armed if peakA1 > peakA2).

  never single snapshots (finding 27). S2b event assertion (st.seededOuter).
  Radial peak/mean gate is cross-run vs the first run of the invocation at

  form mis-fires on legitimate profile sharpening (gfb=0 itself runs x1.09-1.15

  crashed after the first 40k study run (before the A/B block); `| tee` masked

## Plan §5 recalibration (amended in place)

The plan's "restore settle A2" premise was wrong twice over and was not run as
written. (1) Single snapshots of the outer band cannot gate: the +-0.04
structure noise floor swamps the seed's contribution (this is why the tool
prints windowed coherent amps). (2) The fresh boundary seed does not raise the
all-class settle amp — its map-rotated phasor (dphi(R) sweeps 0.93->2.44 rad
over R 2->5.5; signature 0.083 at angle -1.87 vs the input law -2.14) lands
partially cancelling against the young response (finding 28): settleCoh outer
0.094 (S2b) vs 0.128 (legacy) at N=40k, while the warm classes drop only
0.51-0.78x. Gates replaced by history-shape: settleCoh >= 0.5x twin (both
classes), lateBirths <= twin, inner late >= 0.7x twin — measured 0.73/0.78x,
1<=1 and 3<=5, 0.241=0.241 and 0.237>=0.236 (PASS at both N).

## Decision + ship

**Internal** (transient winding of the S2 field) per the plan's rule → ship S2b,
keep gfb=0 (the live term is not the driver and must not chase these epochs;
retire the tau/cap tuning branch). S2b = galaxy.js P.alignSplit=3.0 (CR):
initStars seeds the inner disk as before (a subset of the validated map), and
the first settle() whose steps reach t=30 (the view start) applies the same
bounded map once to R > 3 (st.seededOuter event). check-ics: max |dR| = 0.319
(e(R)*R peak predicted 0.318). align=0 path executes no changed code (bit-
identical legacy trajectories).

Renders (finding 20 decides): render-png calm (S2b) vs SPLIT=0 (legacy) at
settle/T=75/T=150. Settle frames both show connected two-arm structure to
R~4.5, S2b slightly cleaner mid-outer ridges, no double-spiral artifact; T=75
and T=150 comparable honest aftermath in both. Ships as default.
