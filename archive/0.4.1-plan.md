# 0.4.1 plan — transient capture: armed-epoch history + settle-boundary seeding (S2b)

Goal: measure the TIME HISTORY of the outer disk's arm response through and
after the settle transient — armed epochs with birth/death times, one-armed vs
two-armed content, and their relation to the S5 live term's phase coherence —
and make the shipped history honest by seeding the shearing outer disk at the
settle boundary instead of at t=0 (S2b).

## 1. Why time history, not another window mean

- Window means over T=75-150 hide intermittent structure: the outer band's
  coherent amplitude is a 0.03-0.05 envelope with duty 0-25%, i.e. a few armed
  epochs separated by quiet gaps. "Nothing decays" is what that looks like in a
  histogram (finding 23 applies to time as well as radius).
- The settle transient is the one epoch where the outer band is measurably
  organized. Any mechanism discussion must start from when the organization is
  born and how it dies — and the current timeline hides the birth: the seeded
  kinematic alignment (S2) outside corotation is not held by the forcing and
  unwinds by shear over ~20-70 tu (curve-precession.js t_decorr), so a clock
  that starts after 30 tu of settle opens mid-unwind, with armed flicker that
  has no visible cause.

## 2. Instrument (experiments/check-transient.js)

- Dense sample window: every 2 tu from settle (T=30) to T=150 (61 samples),
  calm preset, bar off, shipping ICs — same run family as check-live-mode.
- Per sample, per band (inner 1.0-2.5, mid 2.5-4.0, outer 4.0-5.0 near OLR
  4.74, far 4.5-5.5):
  - A2: mass-weighted m=2 phasor amplitude in the pattern frame (spiral
    winding law, disk classes) — the standard aperture (findings 12, 23).
  - A1: the same for an m=1 phasor in the pattern frame (no winding term) —
    one-armed content.
- Armed sample: A2 > 0.08 (the check-live-mode duty threshold).
- Epochs: maximal armed runs with gaps of <= 1 unarmed sample (2 tu) allowed
  (the envelope flickers at the shot-noise scale; birth/death are +- 2 tu).
  Per epoch: birth, death, duration, peak A2, peak A1, and a classification
  (m1-dominant vs m2-dominant at the peak).
- Live-term coherence at R 4.5 (live bin 17): the table's pattern-frame phasor
  amp*e^(i theta) per sample, temporal coherence over a 12-tu sliding window
  (|mean z| / mean |z|). Correlate with the armed series: mean coherence of
  armed vs unarmed samples and Pearson r(C, A2).
- Noise floor (finding 5): every config at N=10k and N=40k — structure that
  sharpens at 40k is real, structure that washes out is shot noise.

## 3. Decision rule (mechanism)

Compare the same instrument at gfb = 0 and gfb = 50 (plus 25 if ambiguous):

- Feedback-driven if: epoch lifetimes/duty at gfb > 0 exceed the gfb = 0 run
  beyond the N-scaling noise AND armed samples preferentially coincide with a
  coherent live phase (r(C, A2) > 0.5 with a clear armed/unarmed coherence
  gap). Then tune the S5 experiment for transient honesty: a slow ramp of gfb
  over ~40 tu (weaker cap or tau 8-20 as secondary knobs). A ramp keeps the
  settle morphology while the wave establishes, instead of feeding a dying
  transient.
- Internal (S2 transient winding) if: the gfb = 0 run carries the same early
  epochs (born at settle, dead by T=75-100) and armed samples do not
  preferentially coincide with coherent live phase. Then the fix is IC-side
  (S2b, section 4) and gfb stays 0 until a global self-gravity term replaces
  the WKB estimate.
- A mixed outcome takes both outputs: S2b ships whenever the gfb = 0 run
  already carries epochs (the shipping default must be explainable without the
  experiment), and the S5 tuning applies only inside the experiment.

## 4. S2b: settle-boundary seeding of the shearing outer disk

- Split radius `P.alignSplit = 3.0` (spiral CR of the calm preset is 3.02 at
  Om 0.30, OLR 4.74). Inside the split the forced response relocks the seeded
  alignment during the settle, so the disk arrives at the boundary already in
  the forced steady state; outside it nothing holds the seeding and it shears.
- `initStars` seeds only R <= alignSplit (S2, same e(R) profile).
- At the settle boundary — the first `settle()` call whose steps reach
  `P.tsettle` — seed R in (alignSplit, 7] ONCE with the same profile, then
  recompute accelerations (the seeder moves stars; leapfrog needs fresh a —
  the same rule as initStars). `settle()` triggers this internally (guarded by
  `st.seededOuter`), so the page's chunked settle and every experiment get the
  same event at the same simulation time.
- The crest azimuth at seeding time is `Om * t + p * L(R)` of the potential —
  the seeder must evaluate the winding law at the CURRENT pattern phase, not
  the t=0 one (at t=0 the two coincide; at t=30 they differ by Om*t mod pi,
  and a t=0-law seed at the boundary would fight the forcing instead of riding
  it).
- `P.alignSplit = 0` restores all-at-t=0 seeding (A/B runs and any caller that
  wants the legacy ICs).
- What the user sees: the "settling…" overlay clears onto a fully organized
  disk (inner held by forcing, outer freshly aligned) and the outer field then
  unwinds BY SHEAR over the measured 20-70 tu. The transient is captured from
  its birth; mid-unwind slices are gone.

## 5. Gates

- check-ics: unchanged bounds on initStars (inner-only seeding is a subset of
  the validated perturbation); plus a displacement bound on the S2b event
  applied alone (|dR| = e*R <= 0.34).
- check-transient A/B (same seed, S2b vs legacy at N=10k and N=40k):
  - view-start organization (coherent outer A2 over the first 5 samples,
    T=30-38) is comparable: S2b >= 0.5x legacy. (A stronger "restore" claim
    was measured false: the young turn-on response dominates the all-class
    outer amp and the fresh warm seed lands at a partially cancelling phase -
    single snapshots cannot gate this at all, the +-0.04 structure variance
    swamps the seed's contribution),
  - the S2b outer history is quieter and no later: no more armed-epoch births
    after T=30+10 than the legacy twin,
  - inner band (1.0-2.5) late coherent amp at T=75-150 unchanged vs legacy
    within noise (the split must not touch the relocked zone's steady state),
  - every run repeats the check-live-mode stability gates (live amp bounded by
    the cap, median R drift < 10%, radial peak/mean within 1.25x of the first
    run of the invocation - cross-run, since patterning legitimately sharpens
    the profile).
- Full suite passes with the shipping defaults (S2b on): check-response,
  check-jacobi (align=0 physics gates are IC-independent), check-live-mode,
  check-no-clumping, check-ics, check-resonances, check-epi-ratio,
  check-drag-collapse, check-rotation-curve, check-perf, smoke-page,
  smoke-archive-webgl. align=0 trajectories must be unchanged (the seeding
  lives behind P.align/P.alignSplit; the align=0 path executes no changed
  code).
- render-png calm (S2b) vs SPLIT=0 (legacy) at settle / T=75 / T=150: judged
  by eye (finding 20). At settle the S2b frame must show connected two-arm
  structure at least as organized as the legacy frame; at T=150 the honest
  aftermath (no fake persistence).

## 6. Acceptance and shipping decision

- Ship S2b as the default IC scheme if the gates pass: the demo's first
  interactive second is the most organized, and everything after it is a
  single honest shear transient with a measurable decay time.
- Ship the S5 experiment unchanged (toggle + slider, gfb = 0 default) unless
  the section-3 verdict is "feedback-driven"; then the experiment gains the
  slow gfb ramp (on by default inside the experiment).
- index.html journal + reset prose describe what reset actually does after
  this change; worklog in archive/0.4.1-worklog.md.

## 7. Out of scope

- Global (FFT/Green's function) self-gravity (0.5.0 candidate).
- Bar-mode alignment (the alignment rides the spiral winding law).
- Re-seeding or continuous IC driving: S2b is one event — "nothing decays"
  stays falsifiable, the transient must die on its own.
