## Measured: what crest-phase seeding does (calm preset, 10k, same seed)

Narrow-annulus m=2 amp vs R (settle / T=150), all disk classes:

- align=0 settle: 0.27-0.56 coherent R=0.9-4.1, then 0.06/0.00/0.12/0.21/0.32
  (fragmented). T=150: 0.48 mid, <0.12 beyond 4.1.
- align=1 (as committed, young seeded at 1.3x): outer ALL-disk amps no better
  than baseline at settle; per-class showed WHY — the young disk is the disk's
  own strong spiral response (untouched align=0 young amp 3.5-4.5: 0.45-0.86
  per annulus, wide-band 0.37 out to 5.5) and seeding it at the crest phase
  FIGHTS that response: young outer amp drops to 0.20 (settle) and mid-disk
  0.60 -> 0.45 at T=150. Deterministic A/B of untouched young stars confirmed
  the deficit is caused by the seeded neighbors' back-reaction through the
  spiral potential, not measurement noise (initial script had a band-boundary
  bug that obscured this; bit-compare settled it).
- Page-level renders are the decisive metric for IC experiments: at settle the
  seeded disk shows connected grand-design ridges to R~4 vs detached clumps in
  baseline — a smooth coherent ellipse field dilutes per-annulus contrast while
  improving connectivity. Confirming finding 17 (Lin-Shu forced ICs changed no
  fade metric): the forced steady state is IC-independent; the value of S2 is
  the t=0..t_unwind morphology, an honest transient.
