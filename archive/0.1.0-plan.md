# 0.1.0 Plan — Galaxy star-movement demo (fixed rotating field, no N-body)

Source draft: `draft.md` (2026-09-22).

## 1. Purpose

Single-HTML/JS canvas demo, 1k stars default / 10k supported, 60 fps, `file://`-friendly,
no build. Every star is a persistent observable object on its own orbit. A spiral galaxy's
features — thin+thick disk, bulge, bar, spiral arms, halo belt — emerge as families of orbits
in one shared, rigidly rotating gravitational field. Nothing is a rigid group: stars stream
through bar and arms, trade angular momentum at resonances, and migrate between disk, bar and
belt by real (collisionless) dynamics. The demo must be numerically stable for arbitrarily
long runs and carry a short in-page explanation of the approach.

## 2. Non-goals

- Self-consistent self-gravity (N-body, PM/tree, Poisson solve) — explicitly excluded by draft; also O(N²) or heavy, and not needed for stable patterns.
- Gas, hydro, star formation/evolution, transient arm generation (swing amplification), colliding-particle "granular" friction.
- Spawn/despawn particle systems (see Decision 3).
- Mobile UI, accessibility polish, i18n.

## 3. Key decisions (answers to the draft's questions)

| # | Option | Verdict | Why |
|---|--------|---------|-----|
| 1 | N-body gravity | rejected | Draft asks for fast math without it; 10²–10³ times cheaper without; two-body relaxation is irrelevant to the effect we show. |
| 2 | Velocity-proportional drag as "cheap gravity" to feed belt/bar | rejected as default; kept as a teaching toggle | Drag is dissipative: it circularizes and shrinks orbits, stars pile up near the potential minimum — the draft's suspicion ("not stable, ends with all stars in some locations") is correct. Real structural transport is collisionless: resonant angular-momentum exchange with the rotating non-axisymmetric field does it without any friction. Optional toggles `drag` (shows the collapse) and `drag+diffusion` Langevin (restores a stationary state) demonstrate both failure and fix. |
| 3 | Spawn/despawn visual particles encoding density | rejected | Draft prefers stable observable stars; a density-wave field already gives time-independent patterns from a fixed population, so spawning adds nothing physical. |
| 4 | Closed-form epicycle superposition (analytic rosettes, no integration) | rejected | Cheapest and unconditionally stable, but closed forms cannot carry non-axisymmetric coupling: no bar capture, no resonances, no migration — the heart of the draft's question. |
| 5 | Chosen: leapfrog integration of test stars in a fixed multi-component analytic potential; bar + spiral as rigidly rotating m=2 Fourier modes (Lin–Shu density waves) | adopted | This *is* the "predefined galaxy field" option, made stable by construction: Hamiltonian, no dissipation, symplectic integrator → no clumping, bounded energy/Jacobi drift, stars observable through all history. |

Consequence for spiral arms: arms are a **pattern rotating at a constant pattern speed Ωs**,
not material. Differential rotation destroys any material arm within ~2 rotations (winding
problem); a rigid pattern never dies, and stars visibly overtake / fall behind the arms —
"realistic orbits", not rigid groups. The bar field rotates at its own Ωb > Ωs ("field rotates
with the galaxy" from the draft).

## 4. Physics model

Units: G = 1, v0 = 1 (flat rotation speed at R0), R0 = 1. Time in units of Ω(R0)⁻¹
(one rotation = 2π time units); the HUD shows rotations. All potentials below have
closed-form gradients; force = −∇Φ summed over components.

### 4.1 Default parameters (all sliders reset here)

| component | params |
|---|---|
| thin disk (Miyamoto–Nagai) | Md=0.50, a=0.50, b=0.10 |
| thick disk (Miyamoto–Nagai) | Md=0.30, a=1.00, b=0.30 |
| bulge (Plummer) | Mb=0.20, s=0.20 |
| halo (softened log) | vh²=0.536, rc=0.50 |
| bar (m=2, Ωb=0.40) | Ab=0.060 (slider 0–0.15), rb=1.20, hb=0.30 |
| spiral (m=2, Ωs=0.25) | As=0.020 (slider 0–0.06), Rp=2.50, sig=0.70, pitch=15° (p=cot 15°≈3.732), r1=1.00, zs=0.50 (thicker than the bar: strong in-plane arms, gentle vertical heating) |
| softening / safety | Rmin=0.02 (cylindrical clamp), rmin=0.02 (spherical clamp), vmax=3.0 (counted, never expected to engage) |
| pattern ramp | tramp=15: amplitudes scale by smoothstep(t/tramp); tests start the clock after the ramp |

Resulting vc: ≈0.83 at R=0.3, 1.00 at R=1, ≈0.93 at R=2.5, ≈0.85 at R=5 — within
the 0.7–1.2 tuning band; κ/Ω≈√2 at R0. Bar CR lands at R≈2.5, spiral CR at R≈3.6.

Backbone (axisymmetric, static), R²=x²+y², r²=R²+z²:

- Thin + thick disk — Miyamoto–Nagai:
  `Φ = −G·Md / D`, `S = sqrt(b²+z²)`, `D = sqrt(R²+(a+S)²)`,
  `fR = −G·Md·R / D³`, `fz = −G·Md·(a+S)·z / (S·D³)`.
- Bulge — Plummer: `Φ = −G·Mb / sqrt(r²+s²)`, `f = −G·Mb·r_vec / (r²+s²)^{3/2}`.
- Halo (hosts the belt) — softened log: `Φ = ½·vh²·ln(r²+rc²)`,
  `f = −vh²·r_vec / (r²+rc²)`.

Rotating non-axisymmetric modes (each m=2, rigid pattern speed, cylindrical R):

- Bar: `Φb = Ab·fb(R)·Zb(z)·cos(2·(φ−Ωb·t))`,
  `fb(R) = u²/(1+u⁴)` with `u=R/rb`,
  `dfb/dR = 2·u·(1−u⁴) / (rb·(1+u⁴)²)`,
  `Zb(z) = exp(−z²/hb²)`, `dZb/dz = Zb·(−2z/hb²)`.
  Inside the bar the potential is ≈ harmonic with a rotating elliptical contour, so
  x1-type elongated orbits exist; beyond rb it decays as (rb/R)².
- Spiral: `Φs = As·fs(R)·Zs(z)·cos(2·(φ−Ωs·t−p·L))` — logarithmic spiral density wave,
  `L = g(R)·ln(R/r1)` with g a C1 smoothstep 0→1 across r1±0.3 (freezes the winding
  below r1 without kinking the force — a hard freeze injects O(dt) leapfrog errors),
  `p = cot(pitch)` (pitch 15° → p≈3.732),
  `fs(R) = exp(−½·(ln(R/Rp)/sig)²)` (log-Gaussian, peak 1 at Rp; reuses the ln R
  already needed for the phase),
  `dfs/dR = fs·(−ln(R/Rp)/(sig²·R))`,
  `Zs(z) = exp(−z²/zs²)`, `dZs/dz = Zs·(−2z/zs²)`.
  Gaussian (not sech²) vertical profiles are used for both modes: same thin-layer
  physics at one exp() each; the difference is far below the demo's sensitivity.
- Post-0.1 upgrade option: Cox & Gould 2002 triaxial bar instead of the Fourier bar.

Mode force in cylindrical components (C=cos 2ψ, S=sin 2ψ, α=0 for the bar, α=p for
the spiral with α=0 while R<r1 because the phase is frozen):

- `fR = −A·Z·(f′·C + f·m·S·α/R)`
- `fφ = +A·Z·f·m·S / R`
- `fz = −A·f·C·dZ/dz`

converted by `fx = fR·(x/R) − fφ·(y/R)`, `fy = fR·(y/R) + fφ·(x/R)`.
All signs are verified by `check-gradient` against central differences.

### 4.2 Rotation curve, frequencies, resonances

- `vc²(R) = Σ R·(−∂Φ/∂R)` over the axisymmetric components at z=0 (closed form).
- `Ω(R) = vc/R`; `κ²(R) = R·dΩ²/dR + 4Ω²` with dΩ²/dR by central difference
  (h=1e-4·R+1e-5); `ν²(R) = ∂²Φ/∂z²` at z=0 by central difference. Require κ²>0.
- Resonance radii are found by scanning R∈[0.2,6] (400 steps) for sign changes of
  `Ω−Op`, `Ω−κ/2−Op`, `Ω+κ/2−Op` and bisecting each bracket (40 iterations).
  ILR may have 0–2 roots; all are drawn. Rings are recomputed when Ωb/Ωs change.

Transport narrative (what the draft asks "how do stars get to belt and bar"):

1. Inside corotation the bar's field exerts a persistent torque; stars are captured onto x1
   orbits aligned with the bar → the bar is *made of* disk stars on resonant orbits, and
   "disk→bar" happens by capture, not spawning.
2. At/near corotation stars exchange angular momentum and shift guiding radius without heating
   (radial migration / churning) → disk stars diffuse outward, seeding rings and belt populations.
3. Stars scattering through the bar's ILR gain eccentricity and vertical energy (vertical ILR
   produces the box/peanut → "inner belt"/thick-component growth) → high-e, inclined halo-like
   orbits. All three are emergent; nothing is forced per star.

## 5. Stars and initial conditions

Structure of arrays, `Float64Array` per field: `x y z vx vy vz`, `Uint8Array cls`.
Allocate for Nmax = 10 000 once; running N is a prefix slice (N toggle never
re-samples: the first 1k of the 10k set are identical stars). No per-star objects, ever.

Determinism: seeded 32-bit xorshift128 PRNG (seed 1234567) + Box–Muller with cached
spare; reset replays the same seed and the same settle, bit for bit.

Populations (class id, share, sampling):

| cls | class | share | sampling |
|-----|-------|-------|----------|
| 0 | thin disk | 55% | R from numeric inverse-CDF table (1024 entries over R∈[0.05,6], PDF∝R·exp(−R/h), h=1); φ uniform; `vφ=vc(R)+0.10·ξ`, `vR=0.07·ξ`, `z=0.05·ξ`, `vz=0.05·ξ` |
| 1 | thick disk | 25% | same with h=1.6; `vφ=vc(R)+0.20·ξ`, `vR=0.15·ξ`, flared `z=0.15·R·ξ`, `vz=0.12·ξ` |
| 2 | bulge | 10% | Plummer radii by exact inverse CDF `r=s/sqrt(u^(−2/3)−1)`, u∈[1e-4,0.999], uniform sphere direction; velocities isotropic `0.55·ξ` per axis |
| 3 | halo belt | 8% | r from dN∝r^−1.5 over [0.8,7] via `r=[u·(7^−½−0.8^−½)+0.8^−½]^−2`, uniform sphere direction; velocities isotropic `0.50·ξ` per axis (near-equilibrium: no post-settle sag) |
| 4 | belt stream | 2% | Gaussian clump (σpos=0.03, σvel=0.01 per axis) around the eccentric inclined phase point r0=(3.0,0,1.0), v0=(−0.10,0.55,0.15); phase-mixes into a wrapping stream |

ξ = standard normal. Cartesian conversion: `x=R·cosφ`, `y=R·sinφ`,
`vx=vR·cosφ−vφ·sinφ`, `vy=vR·sinφ+vφ·cosφ`.

After sampling, integrate `tsettle=25` time units at dt=0.02: the first 15 grow the
patterns adiabatically via the ramp, the last 10 phase-mix the capture transient at
full strength, so observation starts on a stationary state. The visible clock runs
from t=0 through the settle (settle runs inside reset in chunks; 1250 steps total).

## 6. Integration & stability

- Leapfrog kick–drift–kick with explicit time argument for the time-dependent field:
  `v½ = v0 + a(x0,t0)·dt/2; x1 = x0 + v½·dt; v1 = v½ + a(x1,t0+dt)·dt/2`.
  Fixed `dt=0.01`; substeps per frame = `round(timeScale/dt)`, timeScale slider
  0.02–0.20, default 0.10 (10 substeps).
- Per-substep precompute (once, not per star): pattern angles `βb=2·Ωb·t`,
  `βs=2·Ωs·t`, their sin/cos, ramp factor, on/off flags. Per star per mode:
  `cos2φ=(x²−y²)/R²`, `sin2φ=2xy/R²` algebraically (no atan2); one shared `ln R`
  feeds both the spiral phase and `fs(R)`; one sin/cos pair for the combined phase
  `cos(2φ−β−2pL)`. `R<Rmin` clamps R and zeroes the m=2 terms (axisymmetric core only).
- Drag/diffusion (teaching toggles, default off) apply once per frame, not per substep:
  `v *= exp(−η·h)` then `v += σ·sqrt(h)·ξ`, h=timeScale. Default η=0.05 when enabled.
  Note this breaks symplecticity by design — that is the demonstration.
- No allocations, no destructuring/closures, no string concat in the hot path
  (AGENTS rule); typed-array SoA everywhere; scratch scalars only.
- No radial cutoffs on the modes anywhere: any hard skip radius makes the force
  discontinuous and breaks EJ conservation (found via `check-jacobi`, fixed by removal).
  If a cheap path is ever needed, it must taper smoothly in both accel and potential.
- Perf budget: sim ≤8 ms, render ≤6 ms at N=10k. First lever under pressure is
  timeScale, second the cheap path, third sprite size.

## 7. Rendering, views, controls (all in index.html)

- Canvas 2D, DPR-aware, resize-safe. Projection: near-face-on orthographic with tilt τ
  (slider 0–60°, default 20°): `sx=x`, `sy=y·cosτ−z·sinτ`; depth cue scales sprite
  alpha/size by (1−0.25·min(|z|/3,1)).
- Sprites: one pre-rendered 16×16 glow bitmap per class at init; per frame
  `globalCompositeOperation='lighter'` + `drawImage` (no gradients/fills per star).
  Trails option: paint fade rect `rgba(0,0,0,α)` instead of clearing.
- Colors: thin = warm white, thick = pale orange, bulge = orange-red, halo/belt =
  blue-white, stream = bright cyan. Bar-captured tracers get a green ring overlay.
- Corotating frame (data-space, tilt-correct): rotate x,y by −Ωb·t *before*
  projection with per-frame precomputed cos/sin (not ctx.rotate, which would be wrong
  under tilt). Shows the quasi-steady field with stars streaming through it.
- Overlays (checkboxes):
  - resonance rings + labels (ILR/CR/OLR for bar and spiral; circles drawn as
    tilt-projected ellipses; recomputed live on Ωp change);
  - rotation curve mini-plot: theory `vc(R)`, measured mean `vφ(R)` histogram
    (12 bins), pattern-speed diagonals `v=Ωp·R`;
  - orbit trails for 8 highlighted stars (ring buffers 256 pts, pushed per frame):
    rosettes, x1/x2 shapes, stream stretching — the "realistic orbits" proof;
  - bar-capture heuristic for tracers: in the bar frame, `|x′|>1.5·|y′|` and R<1.8
    → green ring.
- HUD: N, fps (EMA), sim ms, time in rotations (t/2π), max |ΔEJ|/|EJ| over a fixed
  128-star subset since reset, legend, "ⓘ approach" summary (§Appendix A).
- Controls: N 1k/4k/10k (default 4k — both draft sizes supported, 4k looks alive on
  first open without asking for a fast machine); sliders: timeScale, tilt, Ab, As,
  Ωb, Ωs, drag η, diffusion σ; checkboxes: bar, spiral, stream visible, drag,
  drag+diff, trails, rings, rotcurve, corot; pause/step; reset (same seed).
  Keys: space pause, s step, r reset, c corot, 1..5 overlays.
  The stream toggle is visibility-only (physics always integrates all stars).
- UI code lives inside index.html; physics (`galaxy.js`) never touches the DOM.

## 8. Tests & metrics

`experiments/` node scripts, `require('../galaxy.js')` (module.exports-guarded),
deterministic seed 1234567. All print measured numbers and exit nonzero on failure.

- `check-gradient`: analytic acceleration vs central difference of the potential
  (h=1e-5) on a 3D grid R∈{0.3,0.8,1.5,2.5,4}, φ∈{0,0.7,2.1}, z∈{0,0.4,1.0},
  t=3.7, bar+spiral on: relative error <1e-4 (absolute <1e-7 where |a|<1e-3).
- `check-rotation-curve`: |vc−target|/target <10% is replaced by band check
  vc∈[0.7,1.2] for R∈[0.3,5]; κ²>0 everywhere; κ/Ω∈[1.35,1.50] at R∈{0.9,1.0,1.1}.
- `check-epi-ratio`: one star at R=1 with vR=0.02 in the axisymmetric field,
  T=60, dt=0.005: measured κ/Ω from zero-crossing counts matches theory within 2%.
- `check-jacobi`: single-pattern conservation (two patterns have no exact Jacobi
  integral, so gate each separately): N=1000, dt=0.01, T=60 after the 25-unit settle,
  bar-only then spiral-only: max |ΔEJ|/|EJ|<2e-3 with EJ=E−Ωp·Lz. Plus a combined
  shadow run (both on, T=30, dt=0.01 vs dt=0.005 from one post-settle snapshot):
  per-star position difference median <5e-4, p99 <1e-2, max <0.1. Energy drift is
  deliberately NOT gated — the two patterns do physical work (verified dt-independent),
  so only the shadow difference isolates numerical error.
- `check-no-clumping`: N=4000, T=100, dt=0.01, post-settle vs T=100: radial Σ(R)
  (20 bins) and |z| (10 bins) L2 <25% (Poisson floor alone is ≈9%; the rest is
  genuine pattern-driven secular evolution), peak/mean below settled +25%,
  median R within ±10%. Plus an axisymmetric control run whose vertical L2 must be
  <5% — this separates integrator heating (≈nil) from pattern heating (physical).
- `check-resonances`: bar on, spiral off, N=4000, T=100: the m=2 response in the
  bar frame over disk stars in 0.6<R<1.9 must have amplitude >0.15 (capture happened)
  and align with
  the bar major axis within 20° (captured stars ride x1 orbits). No gate on ring
  radii: test particles without self-gravity make only weak rings, and the bar's
  own quadrupole peaks at the bar body, not at a resonance.
- `check-drag-collapse` (documentation test): η=0.05, N=1000, T=50: median R drops
  >30% vs the Hamiltonian control.
- `check-perf` (informational, no gate): N=10k, 10 substeps: prints sim ms/step and
  extrapolates to a 60 fps budget; warns if >8 ms.

Browser smoke checklist (manual) at each milestone: file:// open, 60 fps at default
N, toggles no-op-safe, resize OK, 10-minute run without visual clumping.

## 11. Risks & mitigations

- 10k drawImage cost — sprites are 16×16 prerendered; default N=4k; fallback path: additive
  ImageData splatting; last resort documented in worklog.
- V8 trig budget — one sin/cos pair per star per mode; ln R shared between phase and fs(R);
  per-substep precomputed pattern phases; if still slow, 256-entry radial tables for fb/fs.
- Time-dependent potential + leapfrog noise at small R — R clamp + speed cap + adiabatic
  ramp; modes vanish at the center by construction (fb∝R², fs log-Gaussian).
- Axisymmetric DF in a bar field → initial transient — settle phase with the ramp active +
  moderate Ab (start 0.06, slider to 0.15).
- "Belt" terminology — halo belts/streams + thick disk assumed; resonance rings (ansae at
  OLR) also rendered by the ring machinery, so both readings are covered.
- Jacobi drift alarm — integrator quality is the single point of failure for "stable through
  all history"; §8 gates it numerically at every milestone, not just visually. Two-pattern
  runs cannot conserve either Jacobi integral — gate boundedness instead (§8).

## Appendix A — in-page description (final wording for the demo)

> Every star here feels one fixed "field": the galaxy's gravitational potential, built from
> smooth components (thin + thick disk, bulge, halo) plus two rigidly rotating m=2 waves —
> a bar and a logarithmic spiral. Stars are integrated on their own orbits with a symplectic
> leapfrog; there are no star–star forces and no friction. The spiral arms are not material —
> they are the rotating crests of the field where stars bunch up and slow down as they pass, so
> the pattern never winds up while each star streams through it. The bar works the same way:
> inside corotation its field captures stars onto elongated resonant (x1) orbits, feeding the
> bar; at resonances stars trade angular momentum with the pattern and migrate — outward into
> rings, or onto eccentric inclined orbits that puff into the halo belt. That is how disk turns
> into bar and belt without spawning anything. Because the system is Hamiltonian — dissipation
> free, symplectically integrated — nothing decays, nothing clumps; the same stars are there
> forever. (Optional "drag" toggle shows the opposite: pure friction piles stars into the
> center — why friction is the wrong tool here.)

## Appendix B — formula crib

- vc²(R) = Σ R·(−∂Φ/∂R) over axisymmetric parts; Ω=vc/R;
  κ²=R·dΩ²/dR+4Ω²; ν²=∂²Φ/∂z² at z=0 (central difference).
- MN: §4.1. Plummer: Φ=−GM/√(r²+s²). Log: Φ=½·vh²·ln(r²+rc²).
- Mode forces (m=2): fR=−A·Z·(f′·C+f·m·S·p·dL/dR), fφ=+A·Z·f·m·S/R,
  fz=−A·f·C·dZ/dz; C=cos 2ψ, S=sin 2ψ, ψ=φ−Ωp·t−p·L(R), L=g(R)·ln(R/r1).
  Bar: p·L≡0, so fR=−A·Z·f′·C.
- Jacobi (single pattern): EJ=½v²+Φ−Ωp·Lz. Two patterns: no exact integral.
- Exponential-disk radii: numeric inverse-CDF table (1024 entries, PDF∝R·exp(−R/h)).
- Plummer radii: r=s/sqrt(u^(−2/3)−1).
