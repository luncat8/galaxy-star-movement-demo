# 0.1.1 Plan — Crisp stable spiral arms (young cold disk + resonance layout)

Follow-up to 0.1.0 (plan + worklog in archive/). User report: spiral structure
still fades and looks noisy next to the textbook density-wave render
(Beltoforion / Wikipedia `Galaxy_rotation_wave.ogv`); plus a request to mine
`demo-simple-GLM5.2.html` for reusable ideas (analyzed in
`archive/task2-GLM5.2-analysis.md`).

## 1. Diagnosis (measured, not guessed)

- Warm-disk arms fade ~55% over 150 tu (spiral m=2 0.25→0.11): librator
  phase-mixing + mild LBK heating. Cold disks fade less (trapped fraction
  higher) and respond 2-3x stronger.
- (R, psi) maps show steady test-particle arms live only ILR→CR. CR carries a
  horseshoe gap (cold stars avoid CR itself); CR→OLR forcing overdrives the
  cold disk into disorganization at every tested amplitude. The reference
  video's full-disk arms are kinematic (unphysical); ILR→CR arms + CR gap +
  outer ring is the honest steady state.
- Visual noise is Poisson (4k stars) + background dilution (bulge/halo/thick
  don't participate in arms).

Rejected with data: peculiar-velocity cooling thermostat (pins arms perfectly
but pumps AM by artifact, drift direction eta-dependent); Lin-Shu-consistent
ICs (capture transient is separatrix physics, not initial mismatch — no
effect); slower ramp (same); bar removal (fade WORSE — bar-driven spiral is
real); outer-envelope taper (outer disorganization is overdrive, not
amplitude-fixable).

## 3. Acceptance

- All experiments green (11 incl. smoke-page).
- Young arms: settle m=2 ~0.52 / contrast ~1.9, T=150 ~0.42 / ~1.6
  (fade <25%, vs 55% before); zero migration (medR x1.0).
- Bar: m=2 0.19, aligned 4deg. EJ/shadow gates unchanged.
- Headless PNG renders confirm: bar + inner two-arm spiral + outer ring,
  dimmer background.

- No N-body, no spawning, no cooler (see rejections above).
