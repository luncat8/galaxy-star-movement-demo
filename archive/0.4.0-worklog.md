# 0.4.0 worklog — S5 live m=2 self-gravity study

Goal (0.4.0-plan.md): implement the deferred S5 lever (small self-consistent m=2
amplitude, SCF-lite) and decide by measurement whether it holds arms outside
corotation. Study script: `experiments/check-live-mode.js`; full raw output in
`experiments/logs/live-mode-study.txt`. Calm preset (spiral-only, Om 0.30,
As 0.06, bar off, shipping S2-aligned ICs unless noted).

## Implementation

- `galaxy.js`: `P.live = { gfb, g, cap: 0.02, tau: 4, zs: 0.6, freeze }` plus a
  32-bin cylindrical m=2 moment measured in the pattern frame (grid fields on
  `st.live`), Catmull-Rom table sampling (`liveSample`), the WKB potential
  `Phi = gfb * 2 pi G Sigma2 / k` with soft saturation at the cap, and the
  gradient-consistent force inlined in `computeAccel` (hot path) and mirrored in
  `accelSingle` / `potential` for the diagnostics. Gain fade
  (`updateLiveGain`), `liveGain = gfb * live.g * spiral.g * (1 - bar.g)`.
- Page: `live m=2 (S5)` overlay, `live m=2 g` slider, key `m`, HUD reporting
  (EJ readout n/a while live), journal entry, approach-text paragraph.
- Gates: `check-live-mode` (new), `check-gradient` (live term added),
  `check-jacobi` (live-frozen invariant added), `smoke-page` (toggle/slider/HUD
  path), `render-png` variants `live` / `live-noalign`.

## Two bugs the gates caught (both would have made the study lie)

1. **Sign of the feedback phase.** First version stored `theta = psi`, which
   puts the potential crest ON the density crest — i.e. repulsion. Measured
   symptoms: median R drifting UP (x1.075) and an artificial hole exactly in the
   arm zone. Fixed by `theta = psi + pi` (a density enhancement must add a
   potential well at its own crest). `liveAmp` profile then peaks where the disk
   actually responds, and median R stops rising (x0.958-0.998 across runs).
2. **Linear table interpolation was not the gradient of the potential.** In the
   shipped-first version the analytic force used the interpolated slope between
   nodes while the potential used the interpolated value, so the leapfrog saw a
   4e-4 relative inconsistency (4% of the live force's curvature term) — exactly
   the class of error finding 1 warns about. Replaced with Catmull-Rom
   (C1, value + exact derivative); `check-gradient` for the live term now reads
   3.1e-9 (was failing at 1.5e-4).

Also worth recording: the first version of the study's window metric compared a
step count against a time (`winStart`), and the snapshot array had a band stride
bug that made the "beyond OLR" band leak the previous snapshot's data. Both were
caught by sanity checks on the numbers (a coherent amplitude that cannot exceed
the per-sample mean, and a far band that moved when the far band was empty).

- The real, reproducible effect is the **settle transient**: the outer band sits
  ~70% higher at settle (0.071 -> 0.121, the same direction at both gains and
  both IC variants). By T=75 it is gone.
- Steady state: inner +12%, mid +10% (consistent at gfb 25 and 50), outer
  +15% (inside the run-to-run spread), duty at/below the noise floor.
- Same verdict at N=4000 (duty outer 0.13 -> 0.38 but coherent amp 0.034 ->

  duty 0.00 -> 0.31, still far from "held"), with S2 alignment off (outer
  coherent +50%, duty 0.06 -> 0.25), and with a scratch probe that artificially
  cooled the outer disk (COLD 0.35 / 0.15: outer coherent 0.037 -> 0.048 /
  0.046 -> 0.050). No combination produced a band that stays armed.

### Why the outer disk does not come back

The feedback is not too weak *as a force* — it reaches its cap (gfb=50 gives
`liveAmp ~ 1.9e-2`, flat in time) and it is phase-coherent. It is too weak *as a
source*: the disk's own m=2 moment beyond R ~ 4 is at noise level. Measured with
`experiments/measure-live-source.js` (committed; output in
`experiments/logs/live-source.txt`) in the units the feedback is built from —
mass-weighted moment per annulus with the potential's own per-class shares,
annulus-local `2 pi G Sigma2 / k`, k = 2p/R — compared with the external spiral
wave `as * exp(-(ln(R/rp)/sig)^2 / 2)` in the same annulus (spiral ILR 0.97,
CR 3.02, OLR 4.74):

    settle:   R=2.73  Phi_self 9.0e-3  / ext 6.0e-2  = 15.0%
              R=3.08  7.7e-3 / 5.9e-2 = 13.0%     (CR)
              R=3.78  3.7e-3 / 5.0e-2 =  7.3%
              R=4.13  1.6e-3 / 4.4e-2 =  3.6%
              R=4.48  2.4e-4 / 3.9e-2 =  0.6%     (approaching OLR)
              R=4.83  3.7e-5 / 3.3e-2 =  0.1%     (past OLR)
    T=150:    R=2.38  4.4e-3 / 5.7e-2 =  7.8%
              R=4.48  2.6e-4 / 3.9e-2 =  0.7%
              R>4.8   3.7e-4..4.4e-4 / ~2-3e-2 = 1-2%

(For reference the inner disk starts strong — at settle R=0.98 the disk's own
moment even exceeds the external wave, 8.3e-3 vs 6.5e-3, which is the S2-seeded
inner response. f2, the moment fraction, collapses 0.42 -> 0.03 between
R=2.4 and R=4.5; the drop sits exactly in the CR -> OLR no-mans-land of
finding 16 — f2 at 4.48 is 0.028 at settle and 0.028 at T=150.)

So the loop is amplifying a moment that is 1% of the external wave into a
*coherent* 1.9e-2 pattern: that is not a wave growing from the disk's response,
it is a fixed weak pattern written onto a disk that has no stationary response
there (and, at one shared pattern speed, no resonant capture beyond OLR either).
Inside CR the same mechanism does work — the moment is 13-16% of the external
wave there — which is why the inner/mid bands improve by ~10%.

Conclusion: the plan's premise — "small self-consistent amplitude is the only
lever that can hold arms past R~4.5 because it makes the OUTER disk's forcing
grow from the disk's own response" — is **falsified for the local WKB version**:
the outer response has no coherent moment to grow from, so the loop amplifies
noise into a weak coherent ripple and nothing more. Shipping decision: `gfb = 0`
default (calm preset bit-identical), S5 ships as a gated experiment with UI.

## Next step suggestion

0.4.1 = **transient capture**, not more feedback. The study found two facts to
build on: (a) the live feedback's only robust morphological win is during the
settle transient (outer band +70% at settle, gone by T=75); (b) the outer disk's
coherent amplitude is a ~0.03-0.05 envelope with duty 0-25%, i.e. exactly the
kind of intermittent structure that "nothing decays" hides in the histogram
metrics. So: measure the *time history* of the outer band (armed epochs,
their birth/death, one-armed vs two-armed) with a dense window (2 tu, gaps
allowed) at N=10k and N=40k, and check whether the armed epochs correlate with
the live term's own phase coherence at R 4.5 (it is 0.94 in-window, but the
probe cannot see longer wander). If armed epochs are driven by the feedback,
tune for them (tau ~ 8-20, weaker cap, or a slow ramp of `gfb`) — a slow ramp
keeps the transient morphology honest while the wave establishes itself; if
they are internal (transient winding of the S2 field), the honest fix is IC-
side: seed the outer disk once, at settle, instead of at t=0 (S2b), and keep
`gfb = 0` until a global (FFT) self-gravity term replaces the local WKB estimate
(0.5.0 candidate: the single-function swap is already isolated in `updateLive`).
