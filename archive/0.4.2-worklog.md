## 2. Phase audit — experiments/check-arm-phase.js

Method (all in one script, ~25 s at N=10k; both N=10k and 40k logged in
experiments/logs/arm-phase-n{10k,40k}.txt):

- A. potential geometry, no stars: brute-force phi scan of `potential()` at
  fixed R, hill and well offsets from the reference azimuth
  `ref = Om*t + p*L(R)` that every script (and the S2 seeder) called "the
  crest". Same for the bar (`ref = Om_b*t`).
- B. align=0 run (the potential's own response): per-class narrow-annulus m=2
  phasors reported as crest offset FROM THE WELL, folded to (-90, 90], at
  settle and as the coherent T=75-150 mean (finding 23).
- C. shipping ICs: the seed's own signature (inner t=0; S2b outer event as
  after-minus-before phasor) against the well and against the never-seeded
  young disk.
- D. bar-only run: unfolded x1 orientation against the bar hill/well.

### Measured

- A: hill sits at `ref` to 0.1 deg at every R and t (both terms are
  `+A cos(2(phi - ref))`, A > 0); the well is at ref +- 90 deg. So "pi in the
  phasor" = the trough.
- B (align=0): inner ILR-CR response crests IN THE WELL at both N and both
  epochs: young/all dWell within +-5 deg over R 1.4-2.9 (late window, N=40k:
  young 5, 0, -1, 0, 0, -1, -5; thin 5, 1, 0, 0, 0, 1, -3). The crest swings
  toward the hill only beyond CR (3.13: -7 .. 4.13: -36 .. 4.63: -64 deg) -
  the phase jump across corotation of finding 12, now with its sign.
- C (0.4.1 shipping seed, alignPhase 0): the inner seed landed 24-35 deg from
  the well (a hill-aligned apocenter law rotated by the seeding map, finding
  28) and was relocked by the forcing during the settle anyway (t=30 inner
  amp 0.375 vs 0.391 well-seeded: 4%). The S2b boundary seed's own outer
  signature was 83-88 deg from the well — i.e. on the HILL, against the young
  disk's response at -34: it cancelled part of what was there (thin outer
  amp 0.306 -> 0.269 at the event). Seeding at the well instead: signature
  -52 deg, thin outer 0.304 -> 0.474, young untouched.
- D: bar x1 zone crest -88 deg from `Om_b*t` = along the bar WELL; 472 stars
  elongated across the hill axis vs 260 along it. Consequence: index.html's
  "bar-captured tracer" test (`|xb| > 1.5|yb|` in the hill frame) marked
  stars on the bar's MINOR axis as captured — fixed (now tests `|yb| > 1.5|xb|`).

### Verdict

The "~pi offset" was a convention artifact: the reference azimuth is the
potential hill, and a forced test-particle disk inside corotation crests in
the well, in phase with the forcing. The disk physics is right; the labels
were wrong. Real content that survives: the phase swing across CR (finding 12)
and the sign of the m=2 feedback (finding 22: `theta = psi + pi` places the
live well on the measured crest — consistent with this audit).

### Ship: seed at the well (`P.alignPhase = pi/2`)

`alignEpicycles` gains `P.alignPhase` (apocenter azimuth from the reference;
0 = the 0.3.0-0.4.1 hill seed, bit-identical: trajectory hash equal to the
pre-change build at alignPhase 0). The two dead "crest-pointing unit vector"
locals are gone. Default moved to pi/2 on these measurements (N=10k unless
noted; renders judged by eye per finding 20):

| gate | hill seed (0.4.1) | well seed (0.4.2) |
|---|---|---|
| inner 1.25-2.75 settle amp, thin/thick | 0.375 / 0.293 | 0.391 / 0.262 |
| inner late coherent (check-transient 0:3) | 0.237 | 0.239 |
| outer 4.0-5.0 settle A2 (0:3) | 0.073 | 0.172 |

| outer epochs / lateBirths / armedTu | 4 / 3 / 30 | 2 / 1 / 26 |

The 0.4.1 A/B gate that had to be recalibrated ("S2b >= 0.5x legacy settle
organization" because the fresh seed landed partially cancelling, finding 28)
now reads the natural way: S2b 0.146 vs legacy 0.111 (warm 0.103 vs 0.052) -
the boundary seed adds to the response instead of fighting it. The transient
still dies by T~66 (epochs T30-42, T54-66): the honest unwind is unchanged.
